His life in brief

Born in Jerusalem in 1534, he renewed the study of Kabbalah in Safed, surrounded by his disciples, among them Rabbi Chaim Vital. He died in 1572, aged 38. His blue-painted tomb lies at the heart of the old cemetery of Safed.

In depth

He lost his father young and grew up in Egypt with his uncle, studying under the Radbaz, Rabbi David ibn Zimra. He spent years in seclusion studying the Zohar.

Around 1570 he went up to Safed, where he taught for barely two years. His disciples, above all Rabbi Chaim Vital, wrote down his teaching: Etz Chaim and the Shemonah She’arim. He wrote almost nothing himself, apart from Shabbat songs such as “Azamer bishvachin”.
