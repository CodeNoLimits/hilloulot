Life

Rabbi Gedaliah Eiseman was born in Warsaw in 1910, to Rabbi Avraham and Sheftel, of a Hasidic family of Alexander. He studied three years at the Kletsk yeshiva with Rabbi Aharon Kotler and Rabbi Yechezkel Levenstein, who introduced him to Mussar, then eleven years at Kamenets with Rabbi Baruch Ber Leibowitz and Rabbi Reuven Grozovsky. He then served nearly sixty years as mashgiach of the Kol Torah yeshiva, called “the dean of the mashgichim”, and died on 3 Tishrei 5772 (2011).

Works

His talks are collected in the series “Gidulei Mussar”, one volume published in his lifetime and another after his death. “Mussarei Rabbi Gedaliah” gathers his talks on Elul and the Days of Awe.
