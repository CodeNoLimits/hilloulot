Rabbi Yair Bacharach

ר' שמעון יאיר חיים בכרך ('חוות יאיר')

Hilloula
1 Tevet 5787
Friday, 11 December 2026
The evening before: Thursday, 10 December 2026

Life

Rabbi Yair Chaim Bacharach was born in 1638 in Lipník, Moravia; he was named Chaim after his uncle, av beit din of Posen, and the name Yair was added after an illness. He served as rabbi of Koblenz, Mainz and, at the end of his life, Worms, and is counted among the great halakhic authorities of Germany. An original thinker, he also had knowledge of mathematics and music. He wrote some forty books of halakha, the best known being “Havot Yair”. He died on 1 Tevet 5462 (1 January 1702).

Works

“Havot Yair” (responsa), first published in 5429 with the help of Rabbi Shimshon Wertheimer, is his main work. He also wrote “Chut HaShani” (responsa of his father and grandfather), “Makor Chaim” (a work on the Shulchan Aruch, lost and rediscovered in the 1970s and published in 1982) and “Mar Keshisha” (principles of the Talmud).
