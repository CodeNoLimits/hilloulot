Life

Rabbi Rachamim Elisha Afriat was born around 5605 (1845) in Sefrou, near Fez, Morocco, to Rabbi Yehoshua Aharon. Known from his youth as a righteous man and an ascetic, he was given the title “Kadish DeRabanan.” At the turn of the 19th and 20th centuries he was a dayan in the court of Rabbi Shalom Azoulay in Sefrou, together with other rabbis, and his signature appears on various ordinances. In Sivan 5682 (1922) he went up to the Land of Israel at the head of his community of fifty-three families, supported by a wealthy man, Mordechai Tzaba. He died in Jerusalem on 15 Shevat 5689 (26 January 1929), at about 83, and rests on the Mount of Olives, beside Mordechai Tzaba.
