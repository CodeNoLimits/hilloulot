Life

Born in Tel Aviv in 1935 to Asher Mendel Halevi and Golda Jadek, who had come from Poland two years earlier, Rav Ouri Zohar was, until the late 1970s, a prominent actor, comedian, screenwriter and director in Israeli cinema. He then returned to religious observance after meeting Rabbi Yitzchak Shlomo Zilberman, and devoted his life to Torah study and to bringing Jews closer to the Torah.

In Hebrew, Michael’s message to the group
