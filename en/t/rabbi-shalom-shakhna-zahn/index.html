Rabbi Shalom Shakhna Zahn

הרב שלום שכנא זאהן

Hilloula
7 Tevet 5787
Thursday, 17 December 2026
The evening before: Wednesday, 16 December 2026
Resting place
Mount of Olives, Jerusalem

Life

Rabbi Shalom Shachna Zahn was born near Mezhybizh, into a family of Ruzhin Hasidim, and bore the name of his great-grandfather. At seven he emigrated to New York, then returned to Europe to study at the Mir yeshiva under Rabbi Yerucham Leibowitz and at Kamenetz, where Rabbi Baruch Ber Leibowitz gave him ordination. He returned to the United States during the war and taught for thirty years at Torah Vodaath in Brooklyn; having gone up to Israel, he founded in the Old City the « Aliba deHilchasa » kollelim, where kohanim study the laws of the Temple service. He died at 101 on 7 Tevet 5773 (2012).

Works

He wrote the series « Ateret Yaakov » on the Talmud, an accompanying booklet « Kevod HaTorah », a commentary on the Zohar, « Pirkei Teshuvah uGeulah » and the responsa « Shalom Shachna ».
