Life

Rabbi Avraham Eliyahu Kaplan was born on 12 Cheshvan 5650 (1889) in Kėdainiai, Lithuania, where his grandfather and great-grandfather were rabbis. His father, nicknamed the prodigy of Markov, died five months before his birth, and he received his name. He studied in Telz, Kelm, then for about seven years in Slabodka, where he grew close to Rabbi Natan Tzvi Finkel, the « Saba of Slabodka », and to Rabbi Moshe Mordechai Epstein, who ordained him. Married in 1920, he was appointed soon after head of the rabbinical seminary in Berlin, where he brought the spirit of the Lithuanian yeshivot and of Mussar. He died on 15 Iyar 5684 (1924).

Works

He wrote many essays on thought and poems, among them « Shaka’a Chama », and edited the Hebrew part of the journal Yeshurun.
