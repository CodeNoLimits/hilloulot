Life

Rabbi Dov Tsvi Karlinstein was born in Jerusalem, in the Shaarei Chesed neighborhood, on 17 Tammuz 5694 (1934), the son of Rabbi Yerucham Fishel Karlinstein. His great-grandfather, who bore the same name, came from Poland to Jerusalem and sat as a judge at the Edah HaChareidis. As a child he studied at the Etz Chaim Talmud Torah, then the family moved to Bnei Brak; as a young man he entered the Ponevezh yeshiva, where he grew close to the Chazon Ish and studied with him. In 1966, Rabbi Yossef Shlomo Kahaneman put him in charge of the Grodno yeshiva, founded in Ashdod. He died on 29 Cheshvan 5776 (2015).

Works

His first book, Ma’agalei Tzedek (on the tractates Megillah and Challah, 5622), gave its name to a series of books; he also published Bekol Shofar, Yalkut Shiurim on Gittin and Si’ach Tzedek (six volumes).
