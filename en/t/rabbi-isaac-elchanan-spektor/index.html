Rabbi Isaac Elchanan Spektor

ר' יצחק אלחנן ספקטור

Hilloula
21 Adar II 5787
Tuesday, 30 March 2027
The evening before: Monday, 29 March 2027

Life

Rabbi Isaac Elchanan Spektor was born in Rosh, in the Grodno region, to Rachel and Rabbi Israel Isser, the town rabbi and his first teacher. Married very young, he studied under Rabbi Binyamin Diskin and Rabbi Eliyahu Shik, and received rabbinic ordination from Rabbi Binyamin Diskin and Rabbi Yitzhak Eizik Haver of Tiktin. Rabbi of Zeblin before the age of twenty, then of Rzeza, Nishvitz and Novogrodek, he became rabbi of Kovno in 1864 and served there until his death on 21 Adar 5656 (1896). Known as the rabbi of all the exiles, he answered questions from across Russia and worked for his community in times of famine and fire.

Works

He left collections of responsa, among them Be’er Yitzhak and Ein Yitzhak, as well as Nahal Yitzhak, novellae and explanations on the Shulchan Aruch (Choshen Mishpat), Etz Peri, written to strengthen those who study Torah, and Ma’ayan Yitzhak.

הרב יצחק אלחנן ספֶּקטוֹר (בכתיב היידי, שנהג בזמנו: ספעקטאָר, בניו נקראו בשם רבינוביץ' - ראַבינאָוויץ), (ה'תקע"ז, 1817 – כ"א באדר ה'תרנ"ו, מרץ 1896), היה רבה הראשי של קובנה. היה מגדולי הפוסקים בדורו וכונה "רשכבה"ג" (ראשי תיבות: רבן של כל בני הגולה). נחשב לאוטוריטה בתחום התרת עגונות.
