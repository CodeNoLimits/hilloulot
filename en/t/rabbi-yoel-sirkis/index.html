Life

Rabbi Yoel Sirkis, known as the Bach, was born in Lublin in 5321 (1561) and died in Krakow on 20 Adar 5400 (1640). He wrote the commentary Bayit Chadash on the four Turim, hence his nickname, and is counted among the leading Acharonim. His son-in-law was the Taz, one of the principal commentators on the Shulchan Aruch.
