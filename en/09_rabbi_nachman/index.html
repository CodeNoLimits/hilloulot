His life in brief

A great-grandson of the Baal Shem Tov, born in 1772 in Medzhybizh. He died in 1810 in Uman, where he had asked to be buried. His teachings, Likutei Moharan, were written down by his disciple Rabbi Nathan.

In depth

Born on 1 Nisan 5532 (1772) in Medzhybizh, a great-grandson of the Baal Shem Tov. In 1798, at the risk of his life, he travelled to the Land of Israel (Haifa, Tiberias). He settled in Breslov in 1802, where Rabbi Nathan became his disciple.

He taught joy, personal prayer (hitbodedut) and the strength never to despair. Ill with tuberculosis, he moved to Uman, where he died on 18 Tishrei 5571 (1810), during Sukkot. Since then tens of thousands come to pray at his grave, above all on Rosh Hashanah.
