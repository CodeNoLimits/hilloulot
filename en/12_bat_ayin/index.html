His life in brief

A disciple of Rabbi Menachem Nachum of Chernobyl and rabbi of Ovruch, he went up to Safed and led its Chassidic community. His book, "Bat Ayin", is full of love for the Land of Israel. He died in the Safed epidemic, on 12 Kislev 5601.

In depth

Rabbi Avraham Dov Auerbach was rabbi in Zhitomir and then in Avritch (Ovruch), in Ukraine. He was a disciple of Rabbi Menachem Nachum of Chernobyl, of his son Rabbi Mordechai and of Rabbi Levi Yitzchak of Berditchev. Around 1830 he went up to the Land of Israel and settled in Safed.

There he led the Chassidim through the plunder of 1834 and the earthquake of 1837. He died in the epidemic of 12 Kislev 5601 (1840). His book, “Bat Ayin”, a Chassidic commentary on the Torah, is filled with love for the Land of Israel.
