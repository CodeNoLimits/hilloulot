Her life in brief

Wife of Jacob, mother of Joseph and Benjamin. She died giving birth to Benjamin on the road to Ephrath and was buried by the wayside (Genesis 35:19). On 11 Cheshvan, people come to pray at her tomb.

In depth

The daughter of Laban, Rachel is the one for whom Jacob worked twice seven years. After long years without children she gave birth to Joseph, then died bringing Benjamin into the world.

Jacob buried her by the road to Bethlehem rather than in Hebron. The prophet Jeremiah shows her weeping for her children gone into exile, and God answers: “Refrain your voice from weeping… your children shall return to their own border” (Jeremiah 31:15-17).
