What happened

Forty days after the giving of the Torah, when the people thought Moses was late in coming down from Sinai, they made a golden calf. On 17 Tammuz Moses came down from the mountain, saw the calf and broke the Tablets at the foot of the mountain. It is one of the five calamities of 17 Tammuz, now a fast day that opens the three weeks of mourning. On Yom Kippur Moses came down with the second Tablets.

In depth

The Mishnah (Taanit 4:6) counts five calamities on 17 Tammuz; the first is the breaking of the Tablets. Moses then burned the calf and went back up to pray for the people for many days.

On 1 Elul he went up Sinai again to receive the second Tablets, and on 10 Tishrei he came down with forgiveness: that day became Yom Kippur. The fragments of the first Tablets were placed in the Ark beside the second (Berakhot 8b).
