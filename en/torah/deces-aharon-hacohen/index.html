What happened

Aaron, brother of Moses, was the first High Priest. He died on Mount Hor on 1 Av, in the fortieth year after the exodus: the only death whose date the Torah gives. The whole house of Israel mourned him for thirty days. The Sages describe him as one who “loves peace and pursues peace”.

In depth

Moses, Aaron and his son Elazar climbed Mount Hor before the eyes of the people. Moses dressed Elazar in the garments of the High Priest, and Aaron died on the summit (Numbers 20:22-29). Only Moses and Elazar came down.

Hillel teaches: “Be among the disciples of Aaron, loving peace and pursuing peace, loving people and drawing them close to the Torah” (Pirkei Avot 1:12). According to the Talmud, the clouds of glory that protected the people vanished at his death.
