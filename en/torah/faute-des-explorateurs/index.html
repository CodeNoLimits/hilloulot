What happened

Moses sent twelve spies to see the land of Canaan. They came back after forty days with a cluster of grapes carried by two men, pomegranates and figs, but ten of them spoke ill of the land. The people wept that night, the night of 9 Av, and the generation of the desert was barred from entering the land. Of the men aged twenty and over counted in the desert, only Joshua and Caleb would enter the land.

In depth

The Talmud (Taanit 29a) links these tears to 9 Av: “You wept for nothing; I will set for you a weeping for generations.” On that same day the First and Second Temples were destroyed.

The forty days of scouting became forty years in the desert (Numbers 14:34). The ten spies died on 17 Elul. Caleb went to pray in Hebron at the tomb of the Patriarchs so as not to be swept along.
